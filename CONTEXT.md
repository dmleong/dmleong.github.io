# Danielle Leong's site

The personal site of Danielle Leong — engineering leader, consensual-software advocate, and photographer. It gathers her writing, talks, and a curated resource list on online harassment.

## Language

### Content

**Recommended reading**:
A single curated entry in the online-harassment resource list: a `title`, a `url`, one `category`, a set of `tags`, and a short `description`. The list is the point of the Recommended Readings page.
_Avoid_: article, resource, link, entry.

**Category**:
The kind of thing a Recommended reading is — one of `article`, `book`, `study`, or `video`. Exactly one per reading.
_Avoid_: type, kind, format.

**Tags**:
Free-form topic labels on a Recommended reading (e.g. `trolls`, `gender`, `online harassment`), used to find related readings. Zero or more per reading.
_Avoid_: keywords, labels, topics.

**Post**:
A dated blog entry written by Danielle.
_Avoid_: article (reserve the everyday word for third-party writing), entry.

**Talk**:
A conference session, interview, or press appearance listed on the Speaking page.
_Avoid_: appearance, event, session.

### Navigation

**Navigation item**:
One link in the site's primary navigation: a `title` and a `url`. Whether it points inside the site or off to another host is derived from the url itself, not tracked separately.
_Avoid_: nav link, menu entry, tab.
